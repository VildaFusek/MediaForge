Feather icons by Cole Bemis, distributed through SVG Repo:
https://www.svgrepo.com/collection/feather-icons/
https://github.com/feathericons/feather

Adaptations in Themes/RepoIcons.xaml: SVG paths, rectangles, circles and lines
converted to native WinUI path data; colors follow the application theme.
The forward arrow is a mirrored back arrow.
